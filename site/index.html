How to use Blockwise

Pick what you're opening from the dropdown to score every block for that business, or leave it on "Search by category" to see plain foot traffic. Click any block on the map for its full score breakdown and the nearby places driving it, or pin two blocks to compare them side by side. Use the sliders under "Tune the model" to reweight what matters most to you, and search to jump straight to a specific business.
